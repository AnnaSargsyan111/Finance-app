## Style
- Short, clear sentences; no word-for-word copies of English idioms; keep the calm, informative tone of the English, never promotional.
- Never "recommend" in the sense of advice: use «առաջարկ» (suggestion) and «տեղեկատվություն» (information), as in the English disclaimers.
- Keep Latin names as they are: Finova, tickers, company names, S&P 500, Rate.am.
